## 1. What we ask of you

You review the items our team prepared for your paper: keep, change, reject, or mark as unsure, and propose others if you wish. If you are the paper's author, we also ask for a full runnable replication package. You can stop at any time.

## 2. Credit

- **Every contribution is credited, per paper.** A contribution is one of: reviewing a paper's items as its author; reviewing them as an invited expert; providing a runnable replication package; proposing items the team accepts. Credit is counted per contribution to a paper, not per item or task.
- **Credit does not depend on results.** It does not depend on how AI models perform on the tasks built from your paper, on whether those tasks are released for development or kept for evaluation, or on how many of the prepared items survive review.
- **Form of credit.** *[For the research lead to choose (O-005); one or more of:]*
  - *(a) Named credit in the benchmark's documentation and in each release record.*
  - *(b) Co-authorship of the benchmark paper for every contributor with at least one completed contribution, with author order set by [the number of contributions / alphabetically within groups].* Terminal-Bench-Science grants co-authorship from one merged task (R15).
  - *(c) An honorarium of [amount] per completed contribution.*

## 3. How you are named

You are credited by name, role, and field from the first release. Your name is linked to a specific paper only when that paper's tasks are published (the development set) or retired from the evaluation set. Until then, a public list linking contributors to papers would reveal which papers are used for evaluation.

## 4. Conflicts of interest

One optional question: *Do you work for, advise, or hold a financial interest in a company that develops AI models (including as an evaluation or data vendor), or in a competing benchmark?* Disclosure is never held against you. It lets us record who has seen which items and assign independent reviewers fairly.

## 5. Confidentiality and AI tools

Please keep the items confidential: do not publish or share them. They are the benchmark's answers. You may use AI tools under the three rules on the Guidance page: your judgment in your own words; tell us when AI helped; never put restricted, confidential, or licensed data into an AI tool.

## 6. Withdrawal

You may withdraw at any time by telling the team. Your reviews, proposals, and uploads are then deleted, and you are not credited for the withdrawn contribution.
